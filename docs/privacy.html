ifo Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

This privacy policy describes how ifo (the "App") handles your information. The App is a tool that displays your iPhone / iPad hardware, system, sensor, and network information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or upload any personal information. All data you see in the App (device model, system info, sensor readings, location, WiFi name, etc.) is displayed locally in memory only, never sent to our servers, and never shared with any third party.

Specifically:

  • Device & system information (model, CPU, memory, storage, etc.): read and displayed locally only.
  • Sensor data (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, barometer): read and displayed locally only; never leaves your device.
  • Motion & fitness data (motion activity recognition, barometric altitude): read and displayed only while the App is running; never leaves your device; not used for advertising, marketing, or analytics. You can disable this permission anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness.
  • Location information: used only to display your current location and to obtain the connected WiFi name (SSID). Data is displayed locally only. You may deny location access without affecting the App's core features.
  • WiFi information (SSID / BSSID): displayed locally only.
  • Network information: the App queries third-party public-IP services (e.g., ipinfo.io, api.ipify.org) to display your public IP address. Your IP address is sent to these services to complete the query, and each service has its own privacy policy. The result is displayed locally only.

2. How We Use Information

All information is used solely to display your device and environment status on your own device. We do not use any data for advertising, marketing, analytics, or tracking.

3. Information Sharing

  • Other than the public-IP query described above (which sends your IP address to third-party IP lookup services), the App does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.
  • The App uses no advertising SDKs and integrates no tracking services.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not collect or store any of your data, there is no retention period and no deletion request is needed — once you close the App, all displayed data disappears from memory.

5. Your Rights and Controls

  • Permission controls: you can review and modify the App's permissions (e.g., Location, Motion & Fitness) anytime in system Settings.
  • Revoking consent: denying or revoking permission stops the corresponding data from being read.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:

ifo 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-08-25

本隐私政策说明 ifo（下称"本应用"）如何处理您的信息。本应用是一款展示 iPhone / iPad 设备硬件、系统、传感器与网络信息的工具。

1. 我们收集的信息

本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人信息。您在 App 内看到的全部数据（设备型号、系统信息、传感器读数、定位、WiFi 名称等）仅在本机内存中展示，不会发送到我们的服务器，也不会与任何第三方共享。

具体来说：

  • 设备与系统信息（型号、CPU、内存、存储等）：仅本机读取与展示。
  • 传感器数据（加速度计、陀螺仪、磁力计、气压计）：仅本机读取与展示，不会离开您的设备。
  • 运动与健身数据（运动状态识别、气压/海拔）：仅在应用运行时读取并展示，不会离开您的设备，不用于广告、营销或分析。您可以在系统"设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 运动与健身"中随时关闭此权限。
  • 定位信息：仅用于展示您的当前位置及获取当前 WiFi 名称（SSID）。数据仅在本机展示。您可以拒绝授权定位，这不影响本应用的核心功能。
  • WiFi 信息（SSID / BSSID）：仅本机展示。
  • 网络信息：本应用会访问第三方公网 IP 查询服务（如 ipinfo.io、api.ipify.org 等）以展示您的公网出口 IP。您的 IP 地址会发送给这些第三方服务以完成查询，这些服务有各自的隐私政策。查询结果仅在本机展示。

2. 我们如何使用信息

所有信息仅用于在您的设备上向您展示设备与环境状态。我们不会将任何数据用于广告、营销、分析或追踪。

3. 信息共享

  • 除上述公网 IP 查询会向第三方 IP 查询服务发送您的 IP 地址外，本应用不会向任何第三方共享、出售或传输您的数据。
  • 本应用不使用广告 SDK，不接入任何追踪服务。

4. 信息保留与删除

由于本应用不收集或存储您的任何数据，因此不存在数据保留期，也无需您请求删除——关闭应用后，所有展示数据即从内存中消失。

5. 您的权利与控制

  • 授权控制：您可以在系统"设置"中随时查看和修改本应用的权限（如定位、运动与健身）。
  • 撤销同意：拒绝或撤销授权即可停止相应数据的读取。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

7. 政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们将更新此页面顶部的生效日期。变更后继续使用本应用即视为接受更新后的政策。
